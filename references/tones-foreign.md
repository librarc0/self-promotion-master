# 🌍 外企调性指南

> 核心精神：**Impact-Driven, Structured, Personal Accountability with Cross-functional Awareness.** 让老外/海归评委看到你的 Strategic Thinking + Delivery Power。

## 关键词库

### 必备英文热词
- **Impact**（任何成果都要回到 impact）
- **Stakeholder**（管理利益相关方）
- **Cross-functional** / **Cross-org**（跨部门推动）
- **Deliverable**（具体交付物）
- **Drive** / **Driven** / **Drove**（驱动、推动）
- **Lead** / **Led**（领导）
- **Champion**（倡导、推手）
- **Scale**（规模化）
- **Roadmap**（路线图）
- **Alignment**（拉齐、对齐）
- **Visibility**（能见度、曝光度）
- **ROI** / **Value** / **KPI** / **OKR**
- **Initiative**（主动发起）
- **Strategic** / **Tactical**

### 行为动词（STAR 框架专用）
- 主导：**Led, Drove, Spearheaded, Championed, Owned**
- 推动：**Pushed, Influenced, Navigated, Negotiated, Aligned**
- 创造：**Architected, Designed, Built, Established, Pioneered**
- 解决：**Resolved, Unblocked, Turned around, Fixed, Tackled**
- 提升：**Improved, Optimized, Accelerated, Doubled, Tripled**
- 跨部门：**Partnered with, Coordinated across X teams, Aligned X stakeholders**

### 形容词
- **Strategic, Cross-functional, End-to-end, Holistic, Data-driven, Customer-centric, Action-oriented**

## STAR 框架（外企最硬的吹嘘框架）

**S**ituation — 背景，1 句话讲清楚挑战
**T**ask — 你的目标和职责，1 句话
**A**ction — 你具体做了什么，2-3 句话（最详细）
**R**esult — 量化结果，1-2 句话带数字

```
❌ 没结构：
"I worked on a project to improve our onboarding. I did some analysis 
and we made changes. It worked well."

✅ STAR 化：
"**Situation**: Our user activation rate was stuck at 25%, well below 
the industry benchmark of 40%, impacting our growth target.

**Task**: As the PM owner, I was tasked with revamping the onboarding 
flow within one quarter to lift activation above 35%.

**Action**: I led a cross-functional squad of 5 (eng, design, data, 
marketing, CS) to diagnose drop-off points via funnel analysis, ran 
12 user interviews, prototyped a redesigned flow, and aligned leadership 
on the 3-stage rollout plan.

**Result**: Activation rate jumped from 25% to 42% within 60 days 
post-launch, exceeding the target by 7pp. The playbook was adopted 
by 2 other product lines, contributing to an estimated $X M in annual 
incremental revenue."
```

## 万能句式

### 自我定位
- "I'm a **\[X\]-driven professional with Y years of experience in Z domain**"
- "I **specialize in / I'm passionate about** X"
- "I thrive in **ambiguous, cross-functional environments**"

### 业绩描述
- "**Led / Drove** the X initiative, **delivering Y result** within Z timeline"
- "**Owned** the end-to-end delivery of X, **achieving Y% improvement** in Z"
- "**Spearheaded** a cross-functional effort with X teams to Y, **resulting in Z**"

### 价值升级
- "Beyond delivery, I **drove alignment** with X stakeholders to ensure Y"
- "I **proactively identified** Z opportunity and **championed** the initiative"
- "The success was **scaled** to X other teams / regions / products"

### 反思/学习
- "A key learning for me was..."
- "Looking back, I would have done X differently because..."
- "This experience shaped my view that..."

## 必避雷区

- ❌ 政治化表达（"在党的领导下..."）——外企不吃这套
- ❌ 模糊的"我做了 X"——必须 Own + Quantify
- ❌ 集体主义过度（"我们团队做的"）——外企要明确**你**的贡献
- ❌ 中文直译"我主导了公司战略转型"——外企听了觉得吹爆
- ❌ 没有 Stakeholder / Cross-functional 视角
- ❌ "I worked hard and tried my best"（努力 = 廉价）

## 三个强度档的措辞差异

| 档位 | 风格 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 保守 | STAR 框架、克制、数字说话 | "Owned X, delivered Y result, measured by Z" |
| 🟡 标准 | 主动用 Strategic / Cross-functional / Scale 词 | "**Spearheaded** a cross-functional X, **driving** Y impact, **scaled** to Z" |
| 🔴 激进 | 大量用 Strategic / Industry / Vision 词 | "This was a **strategic, industry-leading** initiative. I **architected** the approach that **reshaped** how the company does X" |

## 完整示例片段

> **场景**：外企晋升答辩（Manager → Senior Manager）
>
> **保守版**：
> In the past year, I led the customer onboarding project. We made 
> several improvements and saw positive results.
>
> **标准版**：
> Over the past 12 months, I **owned** the end-to-end revamp of our 
> customer onboarding journey.
>
> **Key accomplishments:**
> - **Drove** a 42% lift in activation rate (from 25% → 42%) by **leading** 
>   a cross-functional squad of 5 across eng, design, data, and marketing. 
>   Exceeded target by 7pp within 60 days of launch.
> - **Spearheaded** the rollout of a new in-product NPS system, **achieving** 
>   a sample size of 10K+ responses/month and **enabling** data-driven 
>   product decisions across 3 squads.
> - **Championed** a reusable onboarding playbook that was **adopted by 
>   2 other product lines**, contributing to an estimated $2M in 
>   incremental annual revenue.
>
> **Cross-functional leadership**: Aligned 8 senior stakeholders across 
> 3 orgs on the new framework through a 4-week structured influence 
> plan, **turning initial resistance into active sponsorship**.
>
> **激进版**：
> I don't just optimize — I **architect growth systems**.
>
> Over the past year, I **spearheaded** a **strategic** transformation 
> of our customer activation engine that **reshaped** how the company 
> thinks about onboarding.
>
> - **Built from zero**: Designed and launched a **new, data-driven 
>   activation framework** that became the company-wide standard, 
>   **lifting activation from 25% to 42%** in 60 days and **drove** 
>   $2M+ in projected annual revenue.
> - **Cross-org influence**: Led a **4-week stakeholder alignment 
>   campaign** across 3 orgs and 8 senior leaders, **turning initial 
>   pushback into active championship** — a model now used as a 
>   reference playbook for the broader PM community.
> - **Scaled impact**: The activation playbook was **adopted by 2 
>   additional product lines** and recognized at the company QBR 
>   as a **top-3 initiative of the year**.
>
> **My leadership philosophy**: Great product leaders don't just ship 
> features — they **build scalable systems** and **develop people**. 
> I'm proud that 2 engineers I mentored got promoted this year, and 
> the playbook I built is now training the next generation of PMs.
